TwinPane

TwinPane is a personal two-pane file manager for Windows and Android, made by one person for his own use.

Privacy

When you connect Google Drive, TwinPane talks directly to Google from your own device to list, copy, move and delete the files you choose. It does not send your files, your file names or your Google account details to anyone else, and it has no server of its own.

Your Google sign-in is kept only on your device (Windows Credential Manager or Android's protected key store). You can remove TwinPane's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TwinPane collects no analytics and shows no adverts.
